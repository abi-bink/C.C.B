Craftsmanship

From concept to finished emblem.

A traditional process, presented clearly and professionally.

  1. 01

    Design preparation

    Your artwork, dimensions, colours and requirements are reviewed.

  2. 02

    Tooling

    A production tool or mould is prepared from the approved design.

  3. 03

    Inspection

    The tool is checked and refined before production begins.

  4. 04

    Forming

    The metal is stamped, cut and carefully de-burred.

  5. 05

    Finishing

    Colour, plating, engraving and polishing are applied.

  6. 06

    Quality control

    Each finished piece is checked before packing and delivery.
